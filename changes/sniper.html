Sniper

Sniper Rifle

Being a class with theoretically-infinite range and powerful burst damage, Sniper is a supreme threat once he has set up in a good spot. Counterplay to the Sniper is often difficult and frustrating as most other classes pose little threat at his effective range and have to somehow get past the rest of his team to engage with him. I don't think the Sniper class is overpowered in a vaccuum and I certainly do not think he is fundamentally unworkable, but I do think the game would benefit from making him less frustrating.

  • Goal:Tone down Sniper’s ability to lock down chokepoints, and increase his counterplay
  • Changes:
    • (-) Added: Shots leave behind thick, faintly team-coloured smoke trails (like the TF2 Classified ones)
    • (-) Damage from charging rescaled from 50-150 to 150-120
      • Charging takes the same amount of time, but the damage goes up by 20% less per second
      • The delay before damage starts actually going up is also increased to 1.25 seconds (was 1.0)
      • Because overheal has been reduced to a flat +50 HP, Heavy can still be killed in one shot even with overheal
    • (-) Reserve ammo reduced by 60% (10 rounds; was 25)
    • (-) Flinching while scoped in causes the reticle to reset in a slightly different location

Huntsman

In my opinion, the Huntsman is a neat concept with poor execution. Encouraging the Sniper to have to engage with enemies is good, but the Huntsman’s unreliability and the vulnerability of the Sniper discourages this in practice, and the weapon does little to punish thoughtless spam down chokes.

  • Goal:Make the weapon better and more consistent at its intended, aggressive playstyle while punishing safer, more passive play
  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: +25 max health on wearer
    • (+) Added: This weapon reloads while not active
    • (+) Reload takes 25% less time (1.46 seconds; was 1.94)
    • (~) Headshot hit detection now uses the system from TF2 Classified
      • On hit, the arrow will send out nine line traces in a shotgun-like cone, and the hit is a headshot if and only if at least one of the traces hits the head hitbox
    • (-) Max damage from charging reduced to 110 (was 120)
    • (-) Instead of being Crits, arrows deal double damage on headshot
    • (-) Added: Arrows suffer damage fall-off even on headshots
      • This is a custom fall-off curve that starts at 768 Hu and ends at 1536 Hu (50% further than normal fall-off)
    • (-) Ammo reduced to 8 (was 12)

Machina

This item is rather boring, and the base Sniper Rifle changes invalidate the existing design anyway (more damage would let you bodyshot people to death again, and I specifically don't want that). To that end, I’ve decided to give it a complete rework to make it feel more like the cool sci-fi railgun it seems to want to be.

  • New Design – Sci-Fi Railgun with recharging battery
    • (~) While scoped, hold to charge and fire a shot
      • A shot takes 1 second to charge and can only be held for a further 0.5 seconds before the shot automatically fires
      • Letting go of the trigger before being fully-charged will cancel the shot and instantly reset charge – only fully-charged shots can be fired
      • Shots deal 100 damage
    • (~) Scoped shots drain Power; Power recharges slowly over time and from dealing damage when not scoped
      • The rifle has 100 Power; shots drain 40
      • Power recharges passively at a rate of 5/second except while charging a shot, and every 2 damage dealt while not scoped (with any weapon) restores 1 Power
    • (+) Scoped shots penetrate players
    • (-) Charging creates a visible laser beam
    • (i) Power is visible above the ammo counter, and under the normal charge meter when scoped
    • (i) This weapon retains its existing tracers as a hidden stat
