I think most people who take interest in the game's balance already agree on these points, but I'm going to explain anyway. Random Crits are disruptive to gameplay because they cause engagements to end abruptly in unsatisfying ways, and they aren't effective at breaking stalemates. Random shotgun spread makes weapons behave somewhat unreliably at mid-close range. Fixed spread being a buff is only true in that particularly high-deviation shots being impossible means you stand slightly further back and still be confident you won't lose damage purely due to spread. I'm fine with this in the case of the Shotgun, and a random chance to do less damage isn't a sensible way of trying to keep Scout in check or whatever. Random fall damage also adds inconsistency and does not have a single redeeming quality in my eyes.

  • Changes:
    • (~) Random Crits, random bullet spread on shotgun-type weapons and random fall damage are disabled by default (including in official servers)

    • Scoreboard

    The Scoreboard is a little janky and a bit inconsistent in how it allocates points sometimes. It doesn't reward everything I think it should, and some things get rewarded more heavily than I think is reasonable. While this technically isn't related to the balance of the game, I wanted to comment on it anyway since it's something that's always annoyed me.

  • Goal: Make Support points more intuitive, and make the Scoreboard a more accurate reflection of value
  • Changes:

    • There's a large amount of text in this section and it probably won't interest most of you, so I've collapsed it by default

    • (~) Sources of Healing no longer give additional Support points, and sources of Support points no longer give additional Bonus points
    • (~) Every 250 Support points increases your score by 1
    • (~) Damage blocked by ÜberCharge or the Battalion's Backup grants Support points (1 per 4 damage blocked)
    • (~) Increased damage dealt by teammates under the effects of the Kritzkreig grants Support points to the Medic (1 per 4 critical damage dealt)
    • (~) Reflecting a projectile grants Support points equal to its base damage (debuffing jar projectiles will instead give 50 Support)
    • (~) Extinguishing a teammate gives Support points based on the remaining duration of Afterburn (scaling from 0 to 100; was always 250)
    • (~) Destroying a Stickybomb grants 25 Support points, or 75 if it's a Crit Sticky
    • (~) Teammate healing from thrown Lunchboxes is credited to the healer's Healing score rather than Support score
    • (~) Dispensing ammo grants 25 Support points per tick of ammo dispensed per teammate
    • (~) Killing a Medic grants Support points equal to 5 times the Medic's Über percentage (was 500 when dropping a whole Über and 0 otherwise)
    • (~) Huntsman headshot kills no longer grant Bonus or Support points
    • (~) Damage dealt to Sapped Sentries by teammates grants Support points to the Spy (1 per 1 damage dealt)

    • Hitscan Weapons

    Most hitscan weapons have a 1.25 second cooldown after firing before the weapon becomes perfectly accurate again (Shotguns and Miniguns have a 0.25 second cooldown instead). For some reason, the accuracy stays equally bad for the entire 1.25 seconds and suddenly becomes perfect again, instead of gradually becoming more accurate as you would intuitively expect.

  • Changes:
  • (+) Accuracy on non-pellet hitscan weapons steadily improves the longer you wait between shots, reaching perfect accuracy after whatever the weapon's firing interval is plus 0.5 seconds.

    • This is mostly just relevant to the Revolvers (except the Ambassador, which already behaves somewhat like this). The other weapons are getting it for the sake of consistency.

    Projectile Weapons

    This is maybe a bit petty, but it’s always annoyed me how projectile weapons have a left/right bias that you need to compensate for up close. Making projectiles always fire from the centre would be ugly and make it harder to shoot around corners, so I came up with this as a compromise that is hopefully minimally obtrusive. I chose 200 HU since it’s the minimum distance at which projectile aim correction works.

  • Changes:
    • (~) When firing at a player within 200 HU, the projectile will be spawned out of the centre of the screen, regardless of weapon or viewmodel location.

    • Explosives

    I've experimented with other people's suggestions to reduce the power of explosive weapons previously, and the results have usually been fun. This change aims to reduce the margin of error the explosive classes have when fighting less durable opponents. Direct hits will be as rewarded as they are now and explosives will still be useful for controlling space and punishing grouping up, but damage falling off faster increases the margain of error for an opponent’s movement in a 1v1. On Soldier, this should discourage exclusively trying to shoot the floor in 1v1s, and on Demo, it should force him to put a bit more effort into sticky spam to do the same damage.

  • Changes:
    • (-) Splash damage now falls off quadratically with distance rather than linearly (see graph)

    B.A.S.E. Jumper

    The B.A.S.E. Jumper is a niche pick. Within its niche, I think it's stronger than most people give it credit for. It allows Soldier access to portable high ground where he can deal damage while being difficult to shoot back at, and or he can turn around in mid-air and leave a bad situation, aided by damage surfing. However, it requires a certain level of skill to take advantage of the options it gives, and it also requires a certain level of skill on the opponents' part for these options to be useful. Despite its legitimate strength, this is an item most players struggle to get value from.

  • Goal: Make the item more applicable while keeping it from becoming too strong within its niche
  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: Parachute can be redeployed multiple times with a 0.5 second cooldown
    • (+) Added: -20% self-damage taken from blast jumping
    • (+) Horizontal movement speed is now capped at 400 Hu/sec (was 300 Hu/sec)
    • (-) Added additional constraints to air control with the parachute deployed
      • Firstly, you will not be able to accelerate above your horizontal velocity cap by any means
      • Secondly, you cannot change your velocity vector's direction more than 1° per tick (turn rate capped at ~67° per sec)

    • B.A.S.E. Jumper

    Parachute

    Press 'JUMP' key in the air to deploy. Deployed Parachutes slow your descent.

    -20% self-damage taken from blast jumping

    These changes wouldn't do much to make the item better on Demoman. I'm fine with this. It promotes an uninteractive playstyle on him, and I think this will fundamentally always be the case.

    Melee Combat

    Several stock melee weapons derive nearly all of their usability from Random Crits because of how little damage they do otherwise. I think being able to cap off a rampage with a Crit melee strike on classes like Scout and Soldier is quite fun and the overall power level of stock melees specifically with Random Crits is close to perfect, but being reliant on random spikes of extreme power creates bad gameplay. I have an idea for a system that replicates the goal of Random melee Crits in a purely deterministic way that I think is more fun. Users of melee also complain that swings are often eaten by teammates since the game prioritises them over enemies for some reason, so I want to address that, too.

    Credit to Sile Animus for coming up with the larger system that I poached part of for this.

  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: Melee weapons now have increased damage based on damage dealt to enemy players in the past 10 seconds
      • This mechanic will be referred to as melee damage ramp-up for short, but still applies to Crit attacks
      • Damage ramps up to 150% with 400 damage dealt, with damage from melee weapons counting double towards ramp-up
      • This effect will be signified with Crit sparks (but not the Crit glow, like what the Steak buff does), with the intensity of the effect scaling with the amount of ramp-up
      • Melees that currently have No Random Crits — including all of Spy's Knives — will instead not benefit from this mechanic
    • (+) Melee attacks now penetrate players, damaging all enemies in the path of the swing, even if a teammate is in the way
      • Backstabs will still hit through teammates, but will only hit the enemy closest to the Spy; if there are no legitimate backstab targets in the path of the melee sweep, it will instead perform a butterknife and damage every enemy in the sweep

    • Pain Train

    The idea of a weapon that interacts specifically with objectives is interesting, but the Pain Train's big problem is that it only works in four game modes and only when attacking. I gave the weapon an upside that applies in every game mode, even those without conventional objectives.

    I also decided to give it to Pyro. The weapon was originally intended for Pyro and I have no idea why they didn't go through with it. I think it would be cool on Pyro and would help them be better at their originally-intended offensive role.

    • (+) Added: Now equippable on Pyro
    • (+) Added: Gain a speed boost when carrying an Intelligence briefcase
      • The Robot Destruction reactor core also counts as a briefcase

    • (+) Added: Increased objective pickup radius on wearer
      • You have the Scout's money magnet (from MvM) but it applies to Player and Robot Destruction pickups as well as money
      • You do not have the Scout's heal-on-pickup
    • (+) Added: Gain 20 health per second while standing on the other team’s objective (their Payload cart, or a point you own)
      • This is the equivalent of a Level 3 Dispenser (like you get from your own team’s Payload carts, but better)
