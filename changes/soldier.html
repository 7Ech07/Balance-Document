Soldier

Base Stats

The Gunboats are often considered Soldier’s strongest secondary overall, especially in higher-skill environments. This is because Soldier takes a high amount of damage from rocket jumping, and the Gunboats help alleviate this and let Soldier be more independent. Transferring some power from the Gunboats to the rest of Soldier would help encourage more diversity in secondary weapons.

  • Changes:
    • (+) Changed: Self-damage resistance increased to 50% (from 40%)
      • This will not affect rocket jump height

    • Direct Hit

    I think the extent to which the Direct Hit counters certain things is excessive. It one-shots Scout even up to the range where Scout needs four shots to kill the Soldier, it deals massive damage to blast jumpers despite being already the easiest launcher to hit airshots with, and it can damage Sentries from infinite range faster than they can be repaired.

    However, the Direct Hit isn't exactly overpowered, so I can't just nerf it and leave it like that. I wanted to do something to partially alleviate the weapon's weaknesses without compromising its identity, and make the Mini-Crit more relevant.

  • Goal: Remove the potential for one-shot kills and rebalance the weapon to compensate
  • Changes:
    • (+) Explosion radius penalty reduced to -50% (was -70%)
    • (-) Only Mini-Crits targets launched by the user’s explosives
    • (-) Ramp-up reduced to 110%
      • Max damage of 124
    • (-) Damage bonus only applies against players

    • Direct Hit

    Cow Mangler 5000

    Every one of the Cow Mangler's attributes is situational in its impact, so the weapon often plays identically to the Rocket Launcher in practice. However, looking at its attributes, the closest thing it has to an identity is 'spam'. Infinite reserves grants infinite spam uptime, and the charged shot is unwieldy up close but can cram the damage of multiple rockets into one when fired from a distance. This is not the most interesting or fun identity.

  • Goal: Make the charged shot useful in direct combat while preserving its all-or-nothing identity and add a more meaningful downside to compensate
  • Changes:
    • (+) The alt-fire takes 1 second to charge (was 2)
    • (+) Charged shot restores ammo based on the amount of direct damage (not Afterburn) dealt to enemy players (not buildings)
      • The first shot takes 30 damage to restore, the second a further 60, the third a further 90, and so on
    • (+) Removed: Deals only 20% damage to buildings
    • (+) Removed: Minicrits whenever it would normally crit
    • (~) Instead of a Mini-Crit, the charged shot has +35% damage and a +35% larger blast radius
    • (-) Added: 20% slower firing speed
    • (-) Afterburn from this weapon shares the properties of Pyro's Afterburn (10 damage per tick and 3 second duration)
    • (-) The building disable from the charged shot lasts 3 seconds and can be removed early with a Wrench swing

    Beggar's Bazooka

    The Bazooka opens up a new style of movement to Soldier that has a high skill ceiling and offers what I consider fair rewards for it. However, other uses of this weapon — using the fast reload speed to continuously spew rockets or dealing massive burst damage when playing around a corner — can be frustrating to play against. The random nature of the projectiles is also frustrating to play with in direct combat.

  • Goal: Replace the projectile deviation with downsides that do not inhibit the user's ability to aim and which push the weapon away from spam
  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: +200% damage ramp-up at close range
      • This gives the weapon up to 175% ramp-up, like the Scattergun, resulting in a max 118 damage with the damage penalty taken into account
    • (+) Removed: +3 degrees random projectile deviation
    • (+) Removed: No ammo from Dispensers while active
    • (-) Loading a rocket plays a sound that is audible to other players, with the pitch rising the more rockets are loaded
    • (-) Added: 25% damage penalty
    • (-) Added: -40% max primary ammo on wearer

    Air Strike

    The Air Strike employs a hard snowball mechanic, which I think are prone to unfairness and bad gameplay, so I will be getting rid of it. I suspect the reason the weapon even has a head-taking mechanic is because it needs to have a lot of rockets to fulful its fantasy, but intrinsically having a doubled clip size would be too powerful. With the escalating clip size gone, I need to find another solutionto that problem.

  • Goal: Remove the head-taking mechanic and buff the baseline airborne performance of the weapon in a way that feels fun but not overpowered
  • Changes:
    • (+) Removed: Blast radius is reduced while rocket jumping
    • (~) Added: While rocket jumping, rockets split into three mini-rockets
      • Each mini-rocket deals half the damage of a regular one
      • One rocket will always go straight down the crosshair, but the other two have up to three degrees of random deviation
    • (-) Removed: Clip size increases on kill
    • (-) Fire rate bonus while rocket jumping reduced to +50% (from +65%)

    Mantreads

    The Mantreads require a lot of setup to use and even then are less lethal than the Market Gardener. They arguably see better use now as an enabler for the Gardener than as their own weapon. To make them properly worth the secondary slot, I'm adding a new mechanic that should increase the weapon's power, usability and versatility, while also being a lot of fun.

  • Goal: Increase the lethality and useability of the stomp while maintaining counterplay to the item
  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: While airborne, hold jump to perform a slam, increasing your gravity and creating an explosion upon landing
      • Jump must be held for 1 second to activate, and then the slam cannot be canceled out of
      • The explosion deals double your fall damage at base in an area the size of a rocket explosion (which also damages any enemies you land directly on)
      • Increased gravity increases your fall damage, and you can only avoid fall damage by landing on an enemy, so be careful!
    • (+) Added: -20% self-damage taken from blast jumping
    • (-) Removed: 75% knockback resistance on wearer
    • (-) Removed: 300% increased air control on wearer

    • Mantreads

    Boots

    While airborne, hold Jump to slam down, creating an explosion on landing (force and damage scales with velocity)

    -20% self-damage taken from blast jumping

    Deals 3× falling damage to the player you land on

    Shovel

    Disciplinary Action

    This weapon is really strong in the right circumstances. It excels at helping support classes or slower combat classes rotate around the map. It's held back moreso by limitations on the upside than by its downside. The result is a weapon that generally outclasses the Shovel, but which I find awkward to actually get value from sometimes.

  • Goal: Make the weapon better at offering spikes of power but worse at whipping specific people around the map, and give it a stronger downside
  • Changes:

    • (+) Speed boost on teammates increased to 3 seconds
    • (-) Melee hitbox size increase only applies to teammates
    • (-) Effect now has a 15 second cooldown that is triggered when hitting a teammate and starts on cooldown
    • (-) Damage penalty increased to 50% (40 damage)
