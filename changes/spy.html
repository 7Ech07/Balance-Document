Disguise Kit

Several disguises are underused because of the forced slowdown — once he's gotten into position, the Spy isn't fast enough to chase his target without dropping the disguise. Disguises also have some other inconsistencies and jank that hold them back unnessesarily.

  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: While disguised, press "Special Attack" to toggle 'Spywalk', which removes disguise knockback resistance and allows you to run at normal Spy speed if your disguise is slower
    • (+) Disguise smoke particles detach from the Spy and stick in place if the Spy cloaks while disguising
    • (+) While disguised as an enemy, Spy is now immune to all knockback (except when Spywalk is active) and on-hit effects

Invis Watch

People often complain that Spy is prone to dying shortly after getting his backstab since the enemy team is alerted to his position and can often burst him down before he escapes. Lowering opponents' window of opportunity to react to a Spy's arrival or allowing him to escape more easily when caught might be frustrating to play against, so I've come up with a way to change cloak that only benefits survival after a kill.

  • Goal: Increase Spy's chances of sucessfully escaping after a backstab
  • Changes:
    • (+) Spy no longer has to wait for the knife swinging animation to finish before he can start cloaking

Invis Watch

Dead Ringer

Iconic though it may be, the Dead Ringer's feign is ineffective. On top of the technical issues that sometimes give it away, once opponents catch on to the Spy's choice of watch, they can just assume any death is a feign and usually be correct. This is why the current Dead Ringer grants resistance and speed. The point is the free disengage rather than the deception. If Dead Ringer Spies could cloak into the backlines like regular Spies and the feign was a secondary ability, my hope is that it would become less predictable and therefore the actual deception would be stronger and there is less need for the buff to be so powerful.

  • Goal:
  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: If the watch is deployed for 1 second without the feign triggering, the user will enter cloak
    • (+) User is permanantly speed boosted while cloaked
    • (+) Cloak can once again be recharged from ammo sources, but only when uncloaked
    • (-) Removed: 50% faster cloak recharge rate
    • (-) Damage resistance, cloak blink immunity and Afterburn immunity from triggering the feign lasts only 0.5 seconds
    • (-) Damage taken while cloaked drains cloak
      • This does not apply when the resistance is active, but does apply even if you cloaked without feigning
      • Every point of damage drains 1% of the meter

Cloak and Dagger

Well-designed maps often have enough ammo packs around for a Spy with the Invis Watch to be able to get into position and then spend a few seconds waiting for an opportunity without having to stop to decloak. The Cloak and Dagger can feel painfully slow by comparison. Generally, I think the weapon being designed as a trade-off of speed for safety locks it into a slow playstyle, so I'd like to try a different trade-off instead.

  • Goal: Encourage a riskier, more active playstyle
  • Changes:
    • (+) Reduced max cloak drain rate to be 25% faster than stock (was 50% faster)
    • (+) Removed: -35% cloak meter from ammo boxes
    • (+) Removed: Hidden 20% slower cloaking speed
    • (-) Added: No damage or debuff resistance while cloaked

Revolver

Spy is often regarded as the worst class in the game, and a big part of that is how highly specialised he is. When he's not able to get his high-priority backstabs, he struggles to generate as much value as other classes. Making him too capable in direct combat would dilute his class identity, but he can afford to be a bit better at finishing people off or following up on failed backstabs.

  • Goal: Slightly increase Spy's threat at short-to-medium range
  • Changes:
    • (+) Base damage increased to 50 (was 40)
    • (-) Damage ramp-up reduced to 20% (maximum damage unchanged)

The YER's current downside is very severe and makes it tricky to use the weapon without the extra cloak from the other member of its set (which I have since removed anyway). I want to try a different downside that targets Spy's ability to get out rather than get in.

    • (-) Added: 35% reduced cloak regen

      • Cloak gain from weapon on-hit and on-kill effects is also lowered
