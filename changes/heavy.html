Heavy | Ech0's Balance Document

Heavy

Core class

Heavy being marginally slower than Soldier doesn't achieve anything. The very different properties of their respective primary weapons create a dramatic difference in mobility anyway. Letting Heavy have a ~4.3% increase in movement speed allows him to get around slightly faster without needing a Teleporter and chase people with a Shotgun or melee slightly more easily.

  • Changes:
    • (+) Movement speed increased to 240 HU/s, matching Soldier (was 230)

Minigun

Heavy is one of the least-played classes in the game. I suspect the two main reasons for this are the lack of playstyle diversity, and the lack of dynamic gameplay. Playstyle diversity will mostly be handled by unlock changes, but I think making the Shotgun a more relevant part of Heavy gameplay would also help, alongside making Heavy more dynamic and increasing his skill cap.

Tomislav

This weapon suffers from being much too close to stock, such that choosing between this and stock is less an interesting decision to make and more mathematical optimisation. Its original identity involved using Silent Killer and faster spin-up (presumably) to ambush people, but with anything close to the Minigun's damage output this ends up being extremely unfair. The weapon was since watered down into what we have now. I want to take the ideas from various iterations of this weapon and implement them in a way that is interesting and creates a weapon with distinct strengths and weaknesses.

  • New Design - LMG-esque weapon that makes the Heavy less heavy and shifts some emphasis from planning and positioning to aim and movement
    • (+) 75% faster spin-up time
    • (+) Silent Killer: No barrel spin sound
    • (+) Does not deal reduced damage for the first second of being revved
    • (~) Uses a different spread pattern with one pellet in the centre, five pellets in the inner ring and five in the outer ring
    • (-) -50 max health on wearer
    • (-) -31% fewer bullets per shot (11 bullets, down from 16)
    • (-) Cone of fire starts the same size as the Minigun and becomes more accurate as it fires, gradually becoming less accurate again after stopping

Huo-Long Heater

The flame ring from this item is difficult to take advantage of and, when it does work, the extra damage is complete overkill at a range where the Minigun intrinsically does 400-500 DPS. Additionally, its other main gimmick relies on the mechanic of another class. This item is fundamentally flawed and in need of a rework.

The goal of this rework is to give Heavy something new. Projectiles on Heavy are a simple but rather interesting concept. A (relatively) fast-firing projectile weapon could be close enough to the Miniguns to feel like it fits the class, but different enough to have its own distinct strengths and weaknesses.

  • New Design – Projectile-based Firework Launcher
    • (~) Shoots firework rockets instead of bullets
      • Fireworks have a base of 70 damage with 120% damage ramp-up and normal (50%) damage fall-off
      • Fireworks have a 0.45 second attack interval
      • Fireworks explode on impact with a blast radius the same size as that of the Detonator, but the explosion will not push the Heavy himself
      • Fireworks travel at 1500 HU/s (Liberty Launcher speed), have the same size and hitbox size as flares, and fizzle after travelling 1000 HU (0.67 seconds in-flight)
    • (-) -60% primary ammo on wearer
    • (-) 50% damage penalty against buildings

All Lunchbox items

The Lunchbox items are very strong. Arguably the main reason is not what they do for the Heavy himself but because they allow him to heal other people (particularly Medic). I want to redistribute some power from the Medic pairing into Heavy's base kit and I also want to make the Shotguns a more appealing choice, so I think Heavy can do without an easily-replenishable supply of extra health kits for his Medic.

  • Changes:
    • (~) If the Heavy dies holding a Lunchbox, it is dropped as a health kit alongside the ammo box
      • Currently, the dropped ammo box restores 50 health (or 75 to Scout) instead of ammo
      • This is because ammo was obtained from dropped weapons once upon a time, and Valve thought it made more sense for the Sandvich to give health instead, but this carried over even after the switch to players dropping proper ammo boxes on death
    • (-) Lunchboxes now heal a percentage of the Heavy's max health rather than a flat amount
      • This prevents effects that reduce the Heavy's max health from enabling foods with reduced healing amounts being able to heal the Heavy to full more easily
    • (-) Lunchboxes can no longer be recharged by collecting health packs

Dalokohs Bar

The Dalokohs Bar has been largely outclassed by the Sandvich since it came out, and is now even more outclassed by the Second Banana. It's rather inconvenient for something that's meant to be consumed pre-emptively before a fight, and as a post-fight heal it's strictly worse than its alternatives. Additionally, the buff has no visual effect. The buff could be converted to overheal, which does have a visual and is a lot more intuitive, so we’ll do that as well.

  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: 50% shorter eating animation
      • Healing rate doubled so the item still restores 33% health total over its duration
    • (+) Changed: Instead of a max health increase, health from this item overheals

Buffalo Steak Sandvich

This item is very tricky to balance. Being locked into melee combat is a huge risk, especially since the Heavy lacks any engagement tools, but giving the Heavy enough buffs to adequately make up for not having the Minigun out would be unfun to play against. The game already has a dedicated melee brawler playstyle in Demoknight (and Pyro's not far off despite not technically being melee), and both of these have entire loadouts that provide options they can take advantage of. I think making Steak Heavy comparable as a pure melee playstyle is a fool's errand, so I want to try something different.

  • Goal: Shift the focus of the item into being a short-duration, situational surprise option
  • Changes:
    • (+) Added: Restores up to 100% health on consumption
    • (+) Added: 200% faster recharge (10 second cooldown)
    • (+) Removed: 20% damage vulnerability after eating
    • (-) Duration reduced to 8 seconds (was 16)
    • (-) Counts as a small health kit when dropped

I expect the other stock Heavy changes to help out with this, but the Fists themselves could do with an improvement to make them more of an actual option.
